The Theme — 7.0

METANOIA METANOIA

From the Greek meta (beyond) and noeo (to perceive) — a change of mind so complete it feels like waking up. This year, TEDxMDIGurgaon sits inside that turn: the moment a belief loosens its grip, a habit breaks its pattern, and a person steps into a version of themselves they hadn't met yet.

Seven editions in, we're not asking our speakers for answers. We're asking for the exact second the question changed.
